My Academics & Career Goals

Navigating my medical studies at Dire Dawa University is one of the most demanding chapters of my life. From studying complex anatomy models to spending long hours reviewing pathology and hematology concepts, my academic schedule requires discipline and continuous focus. Being a student is about more than just passing exams—it’s about preparing myself to carry real responsibility in healthcare. My long-term goal is to finish my clinical training, gain strong hands-on expertise, and build a career that gives me financial independence, professional pride, and the ability to support my family directly.
