Academic Journey & Career Goals

Pursuing medicine at Dire Dawa University is one of the most intense, demanding, and eye-opening experiences of my life. Studying medical sciences isn't just about memorizing textbooks—it requires understanding complex human systems, working through long study hours, and staying disciplined even when exhausted.

To master tough topics in cardiac anatomy, hematology, and pathology, I often rely on structured video breakdowns from channels like Ninja Nerd and Dr. Najeeb. Studying step-by-step helps me build a deep clinical foundation rather than just cramming for exams.

When I step onto the hospital floor during clinical rotations, the reality of patient care hits home. Seeing real patients reminds me why I chose this path: to acquire real skills, practice with diagnostic precision, and make a tangible difference in people's lives.

Beyond lectures, I also push myself by participating in campus broadcast projects and student activities. I want to build strong communication, leadership, and media skills so that when I graduate, I am fully equipped to build an independent, impactful career and support my family directly.
